About us

Where nature and creativity meet.

Palashvan Acres was envisioned as an eco-luxury retreat where modern travelers can reconnect with themselves, with one another, and with the natural world.

Our story

Inspired by the quiet elegance of Rajasthan and the restorative calm of nature, Palashvan Acres is designed for those who seek more than a routine getaway. We blend thoughtful hospitality, soulful spaces, and creative experiences into every stay.

Our philosophy

We believe luxury should feel personal, warm, and meaningful. Every detail is chosen to feel intentional — from the textures of natural materials to the rhythm of our experiences.

What makes us different

  • Luxury rooted in nature
  • Creative, restorative experiences
  • Thoughtful family and couple hospitality
  • Warm, bespoke concierge service

Our promise

We aim to create a slow-burn kind of luxury — where conversations last longer, evenings feel slower, and every guest feels welcomed with care and intention.
